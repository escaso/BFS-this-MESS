## Tester 1: Rachel Yang

Tester 1 was an junior MIT undergrad student in course 6-4. This app was mainly built for MIT undergrads, so she is within the scope of the intended audience.

She was able to get through most of the screens unaided, but there were a few moments where I had to give her hints/nudges. The first was when she was inputing her classes in Task 2: she was confused about whether “next semester” was the same and saving and locking and I suggested she try next semester (the least intuitive option), which eventually took her back to fill that section in.

More problematic areas were found in the “Roads” UI section. During task 4, they were overwhelmed by the amount of classes assigned to the first unlocked semesters and were confused. I had to point out the cap at the bottom of the semester for them to realize they should edit constraints.

In Task 5, can only see the compare paths sections and is trying to interpret it but is confused by the terminology used. I suggest seeing what happens if they click on a semester, which then helped them understand how conflicts worked.

Lastly, when logging back in, whe forgot her password and was locked out of her app because I did not include a Forgot Password option.

Overall, this user was very confused during the week set up. They were trying to add slots with the details section instead of selecting. It also seemed like she skimmed a lot of the information, like the hour breakdown, that could have maybe been more useful if placed elsewhere.
