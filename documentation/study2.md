## Tester 2: Katie Kudela

Tester 2 was a senior MIT undergrad student in course 8. This app was mainly built for MIT undergrads, so she is within the scope of the intended audience.

This tester was not as good at talking out loud, not as technical, but she was able to get through most tasks without help. I had to nudge her one time in the “week” tab, because she was verbalizing that she was trying to add a category but could not find it and was resorting to “Other”. I added that it was possible, and encouraged her to look through the page from to top to bottom (the add category part is at the bottom of the screen).

I also had to give her the same hint I gave Tester 1 in the “Roads” section. They were both overwhelmed by the class distribution, so I suggested she tried to add a cap to the semesters, mentioned that that was possible. However it wasn’t clear that that meant clicking the “Edit constraints” tab.

This user noted that the app’s weakest point was organization. There was too much text and options in one page and not enough buttons to click on. She liked the concept of the Weekly spread and the Share feature. She also played around with it a lot less and didn’t make as much use of pinning and locking as my first tester did. She missed features like searching for a HASS through the roads section.

Thought the app would be well suited for first-years who need help brainstorming.
